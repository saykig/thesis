# Thesis Research

*A repo for my MA thesis exploration.*

I am still choosing a topic. This repository helps me remember what has
interested me and why each direction might matter. I have not selected a final
research question or a model. Older files are dated snapshots of exploration,
even when their wording sounds more decided.

## Interests so far

Dates mark when an interest was recorded here, not necessarily when it began.
Each link leads to the fuller thinking, so I can return to it when choosing
what fits. These are overlapping areas of curiosity, not parts of one
required research question.

## Where things belong

[Interest notes](docs/interests/README.md) record questions and motivations
without choosing a thesis. [Archived experiments](docs/archive/2026-09-21-information-cooperation/README.md)
keep the evidence, methods, and conclusions from a particular question together.
Future research runs can have their own experiment folders. An archived result
can be revisited, but it does not become the paper's general evidence base.
