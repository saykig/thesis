# Thesis Research

*A repo for my MA thesis exploration.*

I am still choosing a topic. This repository helps me remember what has
interested me and why each direction might matter. I have not selected a final
research question or a model. Older files are dated snapshots of exploration,
even when their wording sounds more decided.

The former repository name, `cooperation-enforcement`, reflected one earlier question;
its case comparison and evidence are kept together in an archive.

## Interests so far

Dates mark when an interest was recorded here, not necessarily when it began.
Each link leads to the fuller thinking, so I can return to it when choosing
what fits.

- **5 November 2026 — Human–AI judgment in military command.** How might AI decision support change officers' decisions under pressure, and what would reliance on private providers mean for European defence? [Idea note](docs/interests/HUMAN_AI_MILITARY_DECISIONS.md).
- **3 November 2026 — Cooperation among European allies.** Why do states with different interests act together, and when does sharing or controlling information help or hinder that cooperation? [Research curiosity ledger](docs/interests/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md).
- **21 September 2026 — Connected information and enforcement.** I explored whether information access changes cooperation and compared four possible European cases. This was one research experiment, now archived; its case rankings were specific to that question. [Experiment record](docs/archive/2026-09-21-information-cooperation/README.md) · [Case comparison](docs/archive/2026-09-21-information-cooperation/CASE_SELECTION.md).

## Where things belong

[Interest notes](docs/interests/README.md) record questions and motivations
without choosing a thesis. [Archived experiments](docs/archive/2026-09-21-information-cooperation/README.md)
keep the evidence, methods, and conclusions from a particular question together.
Future research runs can have their own experiment folders. An archived result
can be revisited, but it does not become the paper's general evidence base.
