# Human–AI interaction and military decisions

**Recorded:** 5 November 2026. **Status:** motivating research direction, not a finding or a selected case.

## Sara's question

> Are human–AI interactions challenging theater-level decisions made by military officers?

Rereading the “About me” page on her personal site renewed Sara's interest in why she studies this area. A Google AI Overview screenshot shared in the 5 November chat sparked the OODA and Maven questions below. Its summary is a research lead, not evidence for the paper.

## What to investigate

- **Decision cycle and judgment.** Does AI decision support change the time and attention officers have to observe, orient, decide and act (OODA)? When is a human approval meaningful, and when might time pressure or automation bias make it formal only? “Maven goes against OODA” and “hours become seconds” are claims to test, not findings.
- **European dependence.** Which private firms supply decision-support systems to European states or NATO operations? Who controls data access, model changes, evaluation and operator training? Ukraine, the Arctic and security concerns involving China are possible contexts, not established cases of European use of Maven.
- **Human behaviour and dual use.** What do psychology and neuroscience actually establish about trust, attention, moral judgment and responsibility under AI assistance? Which results transfer to command settings? How do civilian, defensive and offensive uses change oversight and legal obligations?

This could connect to the repository's information and cooperation question if a documented episode shows that a provider or state controlled a consequential information channel. That link still needs evidence; a faster interface by itself does not establish a change in coalition bargaining or military outcomes.

## Starting sources and limits

- [U.S. Department of Defense contract notice (2024)](https://www.defense.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/3910169/) confirms a U.S. Army licence for Palantir's Maven Smart System. It does not establish adoption by European states or the system's effect on officers' decisions.
- [Ingvild Bode, *Human-Machine Interaction and Human Agency in the Military Domain* (2025)](https://www.cigionline.org/publications/human-machine-interaction-and-human-agency-in-the-military-domain/) offers the concept of distributed agency. Its argument helps formulate questions; it is not evidence of a specific Maven outcome.
- [ICRC, *Decisions, Decisions, Decisions* (2024)](https://www.icrc.org/en/publication/decisions-decisions-decisions-computation-and-artificial-intelligence-military-decision) distinguishes decision-support tools from the human decisions they influence and discusses limits relevant to the use of force.
- [European Defence Agency, *Trustworthiness for AI in Defence* (2025)](https://eda.europa.eu/docs/default-source/brochures/taid-white-paper-final-09052025.pdf) proposes evaluation of reliability, transparency and human oversight in defence applications.
- [EU AI Act, Article 2(3)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng) excludes systems used exclusively for military, defence or national-security purposes from that Act's scope. Mixed civilian uses need separate analysis; this exclusion does not remove other applicable law.

**Next discriminating step:** identify one European decision-support deployment and a decision episode with evidence of how officers used, challenged or relied on its outputs. Separate observed behaviour from vendor claims, institutional announcements and proposed risks.
