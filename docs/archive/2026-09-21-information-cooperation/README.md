# Archived research experiment: connected information and cooperation

**Run:** 21 September 2026. **Archived:** 5 November 2026, when Sara clarified
that this question should no longer organize the repository. This is a record
of one exploratory run, not the selected thesis question, case, or method.

## Question behind this run

> How do connected information structures determine the incentives or
> enforcement required to sustain cooperation?

The run compared whether this question could be studied through four European
settings: Ukraine's access to commercial imagery, EU AI documentation,
Nordic/High North warning and authority arrangements, and EU Digital Services
Act researcher access to platform data. These were candidate cases *for this
question*. Their presence here does not make them four independently chosen
thesis topics. The [High North and Arctic interest](../../interests/HIGH_NORTH_ARCTIC.md)
also exists outside this run; the evidence gathered here does not define
every possible question about that area.
